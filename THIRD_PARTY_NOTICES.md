# Third-party dependencies

TomatoPGFM interoperates with PyTorch, mamba-ssm, NumPy, SciPy,
scikit-learn, Transformers, PEFT, pandas, PyYAML and minigraph. These projects
retain their own copyrights and license terms. No third-party model weights or
source genome assemblies are included in this GitHub package.

The external evaluation scripts can load DNABERT-2 and PlantDNAMamba from their
respective upstream repositories. Their model cards and licenses govern those
downloads; TomatoPGFM does not redistribute them.
